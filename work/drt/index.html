DRT

Voula Evangelia [ graphic designer · logo · merch · print · social media · video ] DRT — Argo

DRT (Democritus Racing Team) is the Formula Student team of the Democritus University of Thrace: students who design and build a race car and compete with it.

I was the team’s graphic designer for the season of Argo, the 2022 car. I designed its logo, the merch, posters, social media posts, business cards and the sponsor videos.
