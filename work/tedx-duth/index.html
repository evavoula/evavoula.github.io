TEDxDUTh

Voula Evangelia [ design team co-coordinator · art direction · visual identity · print · merch · social · motion ] TEDxDUTh — Redefine

TEDxDUTh is the independently organised TED event of the Democritus University of Thrace, in Xanthi — run entirely by students, with its own speakers, workshops and performances every year.

For the 2022 season, themed REDEFINE, I co-coordinated the design team. Christos Komianos and I set the art direction for the year; from there, I designed the identity and everything that carried it — the logo, the posters, the speaker campaign, the booklets, the merch and the social media.
