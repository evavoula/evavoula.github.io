Olfabooth

Voula Evangelia [ presentation design · booth design · ai imagery & video · edit ] Olfabooth

L’ORÉAL BRANDSTORM is L’Oréal’s global innovation competition for students, played in teams of three. The 2026 mission, set with L’Oréal Luxe, was to craft the future of luxury fragrance — new products and experiences built on creativity, technology and personal service.

OLFABOOTH is our answer: a booth where you simply tell a memory out loud, and leave with the fragrance that belongs to it. Team Hesperides reached the Greek national final, as one of the four best teams in the country.

The team. The three of us — Dimitra, Marina and I — shaped the booth concept together. Dimitra wrote a large part of the script and Marina cut the first video edit seen in the presentation. I designed the entire presentation, designed the booth, and created the AI images and videos throughout.

the idea

A memory has a scent

why nostalgia

Scent is the fastest path back

The world feels accelerated, and uncertainty drives us to seek comfort in what we remember. Scent is the sense most directly tied to memory. So instead of choosing a fragrance from a shelf, what if your own memory chose it?

the experience

Tell a story. Leave with a scent.

inside the booth
  1. Tell your story. Share your favourite memory out loud.
  2. Let the booth do the rest. The system decodes your emotion, rhythm and context.
  3. Your memory becomes scent. A real-time match with a YSL fragrance — released as a micro-dose to try on the spot.

And the choice, as always, is yours: you leave with a card that carries your memory, the fragrance and where to find it.

how it understands you

Decode · understand · match

Emotional decoding — vocal prosody analysis reads rhythm, pitch and tremor.
Contextual intelligence — language models work out the “where” and “when” of the story.
The olfactory match — a vector database with RAG maps that emotional picture against the DNA of every YSL fragrance.

the booth

An object you step into

design

Modular, warm, quiet

A soft, enclosing shell of brushed metal that shuts out the noise of a store, with warm light inside and a single seat around a glowing dome. It travels: from a department store to a rooftop in Paris or a seafront at dusk.

Built for impact, designed for longevity: modular, recyclable and zero-waste; powered by renewable, low-consumption energy; with refillable fragrance pods and plantable seed-paper cards.

beyond the booth

A memory, from anywhere

the digital experience

A global journey of connection

Share a memory from home and discover your YSL fragrance anywhere in the world, through the app — the same ritual, in your hand.

the campaign

“What’s your scent?”

Every visit ends with a card: your memory, in your words, next to the fragrance it became. It is the object people photograph and share — so the campaign is built around it, from user-generated videos at the booth to a “What’s your scent?” series set in cities around the world.
